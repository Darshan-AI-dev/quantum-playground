Privacy Policy

Quantum Playground for iPhone, iPad and Mac · Effective 9 October 2026

In short: Quantum Playground does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it works without an internet connection.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

To remember your place, the app saves a small amount of information only on your own device: which episodes you have watched, where you paused a video, your preferred playback speed, and whether you have already seen the opening trailer. This information never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Internet, third parties and tracking

All of the app's content, including its videos, is built into the app. The app makes no network requests, contains no third-party code for advertising or analytics, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
