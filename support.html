Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea for the next episode? Email quantumplaygroundapp@gmail.com and include your device (for example iPhone 16 or MacBook Air) and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

Does it need the internet?
Only for the video episodes, which stream so the app stays small. Everything else, including the opening trailer, works offline. If an episode won't load, check your connection and tap “Try again”.
Videos play without sound.
The opening trailer starts muted; tap “Tap for sound”. For episodes, check your volume and that your device isn't connected to a muted Bluetooth speaker.
How do I see the opening trailer again?
Open Watch & learn and play Quantum Playground: the trailer under Bonus videos.
How do I reset my watched episodes?
Your progress is saved only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets it.
